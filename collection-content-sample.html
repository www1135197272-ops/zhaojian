WORKING SAMPLE · FIVE OBJECTS

Five objects, two languages, a closer look.

A demonstration of short collection labels and image descriptions, using five public-domain collection images from Centraal Museum Utrecht.

Independent sample by Vincent So. Not commissioned, reviewed or endorsed by the museum. Draft translations, object facts and image descriptions require museum and qualified language review before use. Some works are on loan; consult each official record for full credit.

OBJECT 01 · 13968

Toward Evening

Tegen de avond · Vincent van Gogh · 1885

Short label draft

In this 1885 landscape, a narrow band of light draws the eye through a shadowed stand of trees. The painting is made in oil on canvas mounted on panel.

Image description / alt text draft

A dark path runs between tall trees toward a thin strip of sunset. A small figure stands in the foreground.

OBJECT 02 · 21782

Twilight (Old Farmhouses at Loosduinen)

Schemering (Oude boerderijen te Loosduinen) · Vincent van Gogh · 1883

Short label draft

Van Gogh painted these farm buildings in 1883. The work uses oil paint on paper mounted on panel.

Image description / alt text draft

Low farm buildings with dark and reddish roofs sit behind a green field under a pale grey sky.

Centraal Museum Utrecht · Public domain

OBJECT 03 · 21784

Still Life with Stoneware Dish and Pears

Stilleven met stenen bak met peren · Vincent van Gogh · 1885

Short label draft

This still life belongs to a series Van Gogh painted in Nuenen in 1885. The museum notes that he used still-life painting to study modelling through colour tones.

Image description / alt text draft

A pale bowl is heaped with green pears; more pears lie on a brown tabletop against a dark background.

OBJECT 04 · 21785

Young Girl in a Garden (Girl Raking)

Jong meisje in een tuin (Harkend meisje) · Vincent van Gogh · 1881

Short label draft

In this 1881 drawing, Van Gogh used black and white chalk and watercolour on paper. A figure working in a garden occupies the centre.

Image description / alt text draft

A young person in a dark skirt and head covering holds a rake beside a large basket; a house and bare trees stand behind.

OBJECT 05 · 22123

Undergrowth

Sous-bois · Vincent van Gogh · 1887

Short label draft

Painted in 1887, this woodland scene fills the frame with trunks, foliage and colour. The museum relates its cropped composition to Van Gogh’s interest in Japanese art.

Image description / alt text draft

A large tree trunk fills the centre of a woodland scene. Short green, yellow, red and violet strokes cover the foliage and ground.

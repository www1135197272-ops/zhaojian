A SMALL FIRST STEP

Five objects. One clear sample.

A complimentary initial draft for up to five selected objects, based on public source material or material your team is authorised to share.

WHAT THE SAMPLE INCLUDES
  • Short English and Chinese drafts, plus an image description when a usable image is available.
  • Source links and points requiring confirmation; the museum reviews all content.
  • A limited sample for discussion, with no publication or system upload.
Further work, professional translation, specialised language review and permissions are separately scoped and quoted.

Your knowledge stays central.

01 / Select

02 / Draft & check

Prepare a bounded draft with sources and flag uncertainties.

03 / Review & deliver

Your team reviews the content; final formats and changes follow the agreed scope.

Rights and sensitive material

Use only material the museum is authorised to share. Do not send confidential or culturally sensitive recordings until handling and access are agreed. Specialist languages and transcription accuracy require a sample evaluation.

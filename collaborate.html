02 / The one-object pilot單件館藏試點

A small project.
A clear decision.從一個小項目，
作一個清楚的決定。

For a team with a specific exhibition or learning use in mind, and approved images and text ready to work with.適合已有明確展覽或教育用途，並能提供獲授權圖片及文字的團隊。

HK$8,800indicative pilot fee試點參考費用

Final scope and fee are confirmed in a written quotation after reviewing your materials. Target delivery: around two weeks after scope, materials and approvals are ready.審閱素材後，以書面報價確認最終範圍及費用。目標交付期為範圍、素材及審批準備完成後約兩週。

  1. 01

    One responsive web story一個適應不同螢幕的故事網頁

    One selected object or artwork, using supplied images and approved interpretation; designed for current phone and desktop browsers.圍繞一件指定館藏或作品，使用館方提供的圖片及核准詮釋，配合現行手機及桌面瀏覽器。

  2. 02

    Up to three interactions最多三個互動點

    For example: detail hotspots, a short timeline or a before-and-after view. Includes implementation of supplied Chinese and English text.例如細節熱點、簡短時間線或前後對照；包括放入館方提供的中英文稿。

  3. 03

    One consolidated revision & handover一輪整合修改及檔案交付

    Design and build, one round of consolidated feedback, agreed static website files and deployment to one agreed location.包括設計及製作、一輪整合意見修改、約定的靜態網站檔案，以及部署至一個約定位置。

Quoted separately: new 3D scanning or modelling, translation, rights clearance, custom audio, complex integrations and ongoing hosting or content updates. The pilot does not include the full complexity of Mirror Grotto.另行報價項目：新三維掃描或建模、翻譯、授權處理、客製聲音、複雜系統串接，以及持續代管或內容更新。試點不包含完整《照見·鏡窟》的製作規模。

03 / A simple process清楚的合作流程

From a specific need
to an agreed pilot.從具體需要，
走向約定的試點。

The first conversation is about your audience, the object and where the experience will be used. A full build begins only after scope and the quotation are agreed.第一次討論會聚焦受眾、物件，以及網頁會如何使用；範圍及報價確認後，才開始正式製作。

  1. 1.

    Choose the purpose確定用途

    Share the exhibition or programme, intended audience, timing and one possible object. Identify the colleague who can approve the content.提供展覽或活動、受眾、時間及一件候選館藏，並確認可審批內容的同事。

  2. 2.

    Review a one-page concept審閱一頁構想

    Agree the story, interaction, materials, fee and approval points. The outline is a discussion aid; a custom prototype is a separate paid scope.約定故事、互動方式、素材、費用和審閱節點；構想頁用作討論，客製原型屬另行約定的付費工作。

  3. 3.

    Build, review, hand over製作、審閱、交付

    Review the working page, consolidate feedback and receive the agreed files. Agree separately who will host and maintain it after handover.審閱實際網頁並整合修改意見，交付約定檔案；完成後的代管及維護安排另行確認。

What your team provides館方需要提供

  • Images and any existing 3D assets you have permission to use.獲准使用的圖片，以及適用的現有三維素材。
  • Approved interpretation and supplied translations.核准的詮釋內容及翻譯稿。
  • One contact for consolidated feedback and approvals.一位負責整合意見及審批的聯絡人。

What I handle我負責的部分

The agreed web design, interaction and development, responsive implementation, browser checks and file handover. The pilot is deliberately bounded so your team can assess it before committing to a larger project.約定的網頁設計、互動及開發、不同螢幕版面、瀏覽器檢查及檔案交付。以明確範圍開始，讓團隊先評估，再決定是否擴展。

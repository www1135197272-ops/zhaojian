What the public evidence tells us

The museum lists Spanish-only QR audio for two exhibitions, alongside Spanish/English labels. It already provides education resources, booking forms, tickets and an interactive map. Official service information ↗

What these prototypes propose

A language companion and light preparation tools around existing services. These are testable proposals, not evidence that the museum needs to buy them. All graphics and learning prompts below are original.

In 1924 this kneeling bodhisattva left Dunhuang. With its 3D scan, murals over a thousand years old and four mirrors, we build it a cave again. Carry a lamp inside and see whom it bows to.

    一室 · ONE SPACE

    “一室千灯，光光相入”：许多灯在同一间屋里，各自照满全室，互不相碍。这也是 agent.space 的样子——许多 Agent，同在一个持久的云端空间里接力。 A thousand lamps in one room, each filling it, none in another’s way — the picture behind agent.space: many agents relaying work in one persistent cloud space.
